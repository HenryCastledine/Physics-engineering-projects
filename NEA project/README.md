This is my project for my A-Level Product Design NEA, where I developed and manufactured an interchangeable multi-head litter picker so that litter could be collected more easily from a diverse range of environments.

This project follows a full engineering design process, which was diverse and appropriate so that all bases of the product were covered, ensuring the developed prototype could address the problem identified. The sections of my project:
- Problem areas (pages 2-8)
- Primary and secondary research (pages 9-14, 19)
- Product analysis (pages 15-18)
- Design brief and specification (pages 20-21)
- Initial idea and design development (pages 22-27)
- Functional modelling and testing of initial design (pages 28-34)
- Development adn analysis of initial design (pages 35-37)
- Ergonomic handle research, development, CAD and physical prototype (pages 38-41)
- Ergonomic trigger investigation, research and CAD design proposal(pages 42-43)
- Connecting mechanism research, investigation and CAD proposal (pages 44-47)
- Connecting head tool research, investigation and CAD of designs (pages 48-53)
- Secondary model and testing, involving laser cutting (pages 54-55)
- Requirements and materials research (pages 56-59)
- Final design (pages 60-61)
- Industrial manufacture and adhesive research (pages 62-63)
- Manufacturing specification (pages 64)
- Manufcaturing diary, production of product (pages 65-75, 80-85)
- Cutting list and costings (pages 76-79)
- Product testing with client (pages 86-90)
- Questionnaire and evaluation of product (pages 91-95)

This project incorporated CAD, 3D printing, laser cutting, iterative prototyping, user-centred design and designing for manufacture, awarded 96/100 (A*) - highest Product Design NEA mark in my school.
