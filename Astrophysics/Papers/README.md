Here are of my two reports for Astrophysics, one for the transit method and the other describing the energy transportation processes that occur within stars.

The transit method report is a report describing the detection of the exoplanet Kepler-17 b of the star system Kepler-17 using the transit method, whilst accounting for stellar variability. This report includes observations, data analysis, the method, discussion and conclusion of the experiment that occurred. 

The energy transport 1-500 word scientific report discussing the energy transport processes that occur within stellar interiors. Radiative transport, convection and conduction are discussed and how they vary within different-mass stars. The mixing-length theory, Schwarzschild criterion are discussed, as well as current research.
